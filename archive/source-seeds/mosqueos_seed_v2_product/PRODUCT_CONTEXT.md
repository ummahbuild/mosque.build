# mosque.build — Product Context for Code Harness

## Mission
mosque.build is a comprehensive mosque lifecycle platform: discover mosques; study mosque architecture; determine community need; choose sites; navigate jurisdictional requirements; budget and fund projects; design in 2D/3D; source materials; construct; open; manage; maintain; program; endow; restore; and document mosques.

## Core product surfaces
1. Global mosque directory + map.
2. Mosque architecture/museum knowledge graph.
3. Mosque project workspace.
4. Three.js + synchronized 2D mosque design studio.
5. Budget, cost, sourcing and construction planner.
6. Permit/jurisdiction research layer.
7. Funding, donations and waqf/endowment tools.
8. Mosque operations OS.
9. Madrasa/community programming.
10. Ramadan, iftar, taraweeh and Eid operations.
11. Security, safety and facilities.
12. SEO/AEO knowledge and local discovery platform.

## Code harness rules
- Read `DEFINITION_OF_DONE.json` and `DEV_PLAN.json` before implementing features.
- `FEATURE_REGISTRY.json` is the canonical granular feature inventory.
- `DATA_COMPLETION.json` defines when content datasets can be called complete.
- Never turn research queues into factual public records automatically.
- Keep field-level provenance for hours, facilities, architecture, prices, regulatory rules and financial case studies.
- Google Places is a live provider integration, not a source to scrape and mirror.
- Keep astronomical prayer calculations distinct from mosque-published iqama/Jumuah times.
- A mosque's office/opening hours are not automatically proof that prayer access is available at every listed time.
- Regulatory guidance must expose official source, jurisdiction, last verified date and confirmation status.
- Three.js geometry/cost/suggestion rules must be deterministic modules with unit tests.
- Any material or design change that affects cost should also expose operating/maintenance impact where possible.
- Money flows require restricted-fund support and reconciliation.
- Normative Islamic claims need a dedicated religious review state.
- Do not label cultural features (dome/minaret/style) as religious obligations.
- Copyrighted plans, images and videos are indexed by metadata/link unless rights explicitly permit mirroring.

## Product principle
Do not only help a community construct a building. Help it create an institution it can afford to operate, maintain, program and preserve for generations.
