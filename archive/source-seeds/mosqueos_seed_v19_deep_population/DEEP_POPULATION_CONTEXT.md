# mosque.build — Deep Population & Missing Gaps v19

This pass adds reusable project data rather than only new schemas.

## Populated defaults
- 25 room/building conversion presets
- 40 parametric construction assemblies
- 30 inspection/test-plan templates
- 20 mosque/building commissioning scenarios
- source-backed construction safety template examples
- explicit launch-data completeness targets
- missing-task register

## Standards/source position
IFC 4.3 is the current official IFC version according to buildingSMART. IDS is used as a machine-readable information requirement format. Commissioning templates are informed by WBDG/UFGS lifecycle concepts. OSHA templates are explicitly U.S.-jurisdiction examples and never global rules.

## Data principle
Templates are not project facts. Regional prices, actual regulations, actual product availability and actual site safety requirements remain project/source specific.
