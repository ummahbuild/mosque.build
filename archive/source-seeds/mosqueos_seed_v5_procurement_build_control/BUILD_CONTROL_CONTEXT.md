# mosque.build — Procurement + Construction Control Context

This module turns mosque.build from a design/planning tool into a construction-delivery and handover platform.

## Product gallery
Users can browse mosque-specific and general construction products, compare materials, request/record samples, collect technical submittals, obtain quotes, track procurement, inspect delivery, install, commission and hand the installed item into the maintenance asset register.

## Construction control
Track:
- schedule and work breakdown
- daily field reports
- progress photographs and fixed-view comparisons
- RFIs
- submittals
- inspections
- change events/orders
- schedule of values and progress payment claims
- procurement and long-lead items
- budget, contingency, committed cost, cash flow and forecast final cost
- snag/punch lists
- commissioning
- warranties/as-builts/O&M
- opening readiness

## mosque-specific QA
Generic construction completion is not enough. Opening checks include qibla verification, prayer rows, wudu drainage, accessible route, women/family facilities, carpet, mihrab/minbar, intelligible audio, madrasa readiness and Ramadan/iftar operational readiness.

## Evidence principle
A contractor-reported percentage, a photo, a quantity installed and a certified progress/payment value are different things. Store them separately.

## Data interoperability
Use open BIM structures where practical. IFC is the preferred neutral model-exchange target; BCF/IDS can be supported for issue/information coordination.

## Procurement principle
A product being listed in the marketplace does not make it project-approved. Project approval requires technical review, jurisdiction/code fit where applicable, cost/lead-time confirmation and submittal approval.
