# mosque.build — Mosque Gardens, Natural Facades & Biophilic Infrastructure v24

This module integrates landscape and vegetation into the same lifecycle as architecture, engineering, sourcing, budget, construction and operations.

## Added
- 189 landscape/biophilic features
- 10 mosque garden presets
- natural facade/green wall/trellis system models
- plant data model and safe archetype seeds
- rain garden/bioswale/permeable paving/cistern/green roof workflows
- irrigation and water-reuse scenario model
- 10 landscape parametric assemblies
- landscape lifecycle events
- SQL foundation for landscape zones, palettes, assets and water reuse
- executable canopy, irrigation, rain-capture, shade and procurement-date calculations

## Core rule
Plants are living assets with mature dimensions, water needs, maintenance, replacement and safety considerations. Render-scale beauty is never enough.

## Natural facade rule
Vegetation remains a maintainable system coordinated with waterproofing, structure, drainage, fire safety and access. Direct attachment to the weatherproof envelope is not assumed safe.

## Water reuse rule
Rainwater/greywater/ablution-water reuse remains jurisdiction- and treatment-specific. The app creates candidate scenarios and professional/regulatory verification tasks rather than claiming untreated water is safe.

## Local sourcing
Plant palettes prefer verified locally suitable/native options where appropriate, local nursery availability, local landscape skill, replacement stock and climate fit.
