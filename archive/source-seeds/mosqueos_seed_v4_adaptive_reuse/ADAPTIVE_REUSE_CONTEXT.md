# mosque.build — Adaptive Reuse + Construction Policy Context

This module handles purchase, lease, inspection, scanning, repair, conversion and reuse of existing buildings as mosques and Islamic community facilities.

## Core principle
The app should be able to take a real building — such as an old church, community centre, warehouse, school, office, house, cinema or factory — and answer:

1. Can the community lawfully pursue mosque use here?
2. What information is still unknown?
3. What is broken or unsafe?
4. What must be repaired before aesthetic work?
5. How efficiently can the building orient to qibla and hold prayer rows?
6. Where can wudu, women/family facilities, madrasa, community programming, kitchen/iftar, administration and storage fit?
7. What code/permit consequences are likely, based on the actual jurisdiction?
8. What will acquisition + repair + conversion + operations cost?
9. Is buying, leasing, converting, rebuilding, or passing the smarter choice?
10. How can compatible rooms serve multiple purposes?
11. What should be preserved, repaired, reused, salvaged, replaced or added?
12. How does the building become a maintainable mosque after opening?

## Never infer
- structural capacity from photos
- permit approval
- absence of asbestos/lead/contamination
- locally adopted code edition without verification
- a religious obligation for a dome, minaret or stylistic element

## Design philosophy
Safety -> weather-tightness -> code/accessibility -> essential building systems -> mosque function -> community programming -> aesthetics/craft.
