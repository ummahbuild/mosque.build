# mosque.build — Engineering, Local Sourcing & Construction v22

## What this adds
- 97 engineering/construction/local-sourcing features
- 10 engineering discipline modules
- 14 detailed construction process templates
- 20 method-statement templates
- new-build/adaptive-reuse/room-conversion WBS templates
- engineering review gates
- local sourcing/substitution engine
- executable JS math + offline-outbox tests
- SQL draft for engineering issues, local sourcing options and method statements

## Engineering principle
mosque.build coordinates engineering information and detects review needs; it does not impersonate the licensed professionals/authorities required by a project's jurisdiction.

## Local sourcing principle
Local materials/craft/vendor options are first-class and can be favored for lead time, repairability, local economic value and design relevance, but hard technical/safety constraints always win.

## openBIM
The interoperability layer targets IFC 4.3 and IDS-based information requirements/checks.

## Construction
The project flow now explicitly covers tendering/prequalification, submittals, samples, method statements, pre-installation meetings, receiving, installation/ITP, changes, payment certification, commissioning, handover and warranty.
