# Third-party and asset notices

This repository contains historical research seeds, external-source references and illustrative visual assets. A complete redistribution-rights inventory is not yet finished.

## Current rules

- A URL or source citation does not grant redistribution rights.
- Product/vendor records are observations, not endorsements or availability claims.
- Archived source seeds are research inputs and may include third-party names or public jurisdiction references.
- Visual mockups under `assets/mockups` and mirrored public assets require author/source and license review before an outbound repository license covers them.
- Contributors must not add copied text, imagery, fonts, product data or sacred/religious content without source, rights and review metadata.

See `docs/NEXT_BUILD.md` for the asset-rights inventory release blocker. This notice is not a substitute for legal review or a final software license.
