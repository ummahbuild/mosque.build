# Third-party and asset notices

This repository contains historical research seeds, external-source references and illustrative visual assets. A complete redistribution-rights inventory is not yet finished. The MIT License applies only to material the copyright holders have the right to license; it does not override third-party rights.

## Current rules

- A URL or source citation does not grant redistribution rights.
- Product/vendor records are observations, not endorsements or availability claims.
- Archived source seeds are research inputs and may include third-party names or public jurisdiction references.
- Visual examples under `assets/mockups` and mirrored public assets require author/source and license review before they can be treated as MIT-licensed material.
- Contributors must not add copied text, imagery, fonts, product data or sacred/religious content without source, rights and review metadata.

See `docs/ASSET_POLICY.md` and `docs/NEXT_BUILD.md` for the asset-rights inventory. This notice is not a substitute for legal review.

## Country flags

Country flag SVGs under `apps/web/public/flags` are sourced from the public-domain renders in [`hampusborgos/country-flags`](https://github.com/hampusborgos/country-flags). `de.svg` is an equivalent repository-authored geometric rendering of Germany's public-domain national flag. Flags identify the country attached to a jurisdiction record and must not imply government affiliation or approval.

## Architecture reference photographs

`apps/web/data/architecture-image-references.json` links to 15 photographs hosted by Wikimedia Commons. The records retain the individual file page, photographer or creator, license, license URL and observation date. Licenses include Public Domain, CC BY 2.0, CC BY-SA 2.0, CC BY-SA 2.5, CC BY-SA 3.0 and CC BY-SA 4.0; the applicable license is recorded per image and controls reuse of that image. These photographs are not part of the repository's MIT-licensed source code, are not endorsements, and do not establish architectural authenticity, suitability, engineering performance or permission to reproduce a building. No photograph is committed to this repository.
