# Community Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, accessible and constructive community. Treat contributors, mosque communities, professionals, scholars, suppliers and public authorities with dignity. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of personal information, and attacks on people or communities are not acceptable.

Discuss ideas with evidence and humility. Mosque projects cross cultures, schools of thought, jurisdictions, abilities and professional disciplines; disagreement must not become stereotyping or claims of authority a contributor does not hold.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@ummah.build`. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, pause participation, or restrict access to protect the community. This document should receive organizational and legal review before a public launch.
