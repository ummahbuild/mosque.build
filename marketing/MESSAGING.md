# Messaging guide

## Positioning

mosque.build is an open-source planning and design workspace that helps communities understand, coordinate, and preserve the full journey of creating and caring for a mosque.

It connects the community brief, site, architecture, accessibility, approvals, cost, funding, procurement, construction, handover, operations, and long-term stewardship in one project journey.

## One-line versions

- Plan a mosque from community need to long-term care.
- Bring every mosque-project decision into one clear journey.
- Build the institution, not only the building.
- Better questions, clearer decisions, stronger mosque projects.

## The problem

Mosque committees may need to coordinate land, community needs, qibla, planning, design, engineering, fundraising, procurement, construction, and operations while working across disconnected files and volunteer handovers. Important assumptions become difficult to trace. Costs appear without scope. Renderings arrive before site questions are resolved. Maintenance is postponed until opening.

## The promise

mosque.build helps a community:

- begin with worship and community needs;
- see what is known, assumed, sourced, and still unresolved;
- understand which decision comes next;
- compare sites, spaces, materials, costs, and delivery options;
- coordinate the right professional or authority review;
- preserve decisions and evidence for future committee members;
- plan opening, maintenance, resilience, and operating sustainability early;
- share reviewed lessons so future mosque projects can begin better.

## Benefits by audience

### Communities and committees

- A guided starting point instead of a blank document.
- One connected view of decisions across the project.
- Clear reminders about missing evidence and accountable owners.
- Private browser-based drafts with explicit export.
- Practical language around complex architecture and construction work.

### Architects and engineers

- A clearer community brief and record of assumptions.
- Earlier visibility of worship, inclusion, operations, and maintenance needs.
- Structured handoffs that distinguish community intent from professional conclusions.
- A shared vocabulary for unresolved questions and review gates.

### Donors and boards

- A visible relationship between need, scope, cost, and project stage.
- Better separation of estimates, commitments, payments, and verified progress.
- A stronger basis for restricted funds, reserves, and long-term care.

### Contributors

- A meaningful open-source problem spanning civic technology, architecture, accessibility, data provenance, visualization, and community infrastructure.
- Clear contribution boundaries and testable work areas.
- Opportunities for code, research, design, documentation, translation, and professional review.

## What mosque.build is not

- It is not an architect, engineer, contractor, quantity surveyor, authority, legal adviser, or religious authority.
- It does not guarantee permits, prices, availability, compliance, safety, authenticity, or approval.
- It does not turn a conceptual 3D model into construction documents.
- It does not treat a supplier listing as a recommendation.
- It does not upload current public project drafts unless the user explicitly exports or shares them.

## Brand voice

Use language that is calm, practical, hopeful, specific, and honest about uncertainty.

Prefer:

- “Record what still needs verification.”
- “Compare options with their sources and trade-offs.”
- “Bring this study to the appropriate professional.”
- “Start with the community need.”

Avoid:

- “Build instantly.”
- “Guaranteed permit approval.”
- “AI-designed mosque.”
- “Accurate local price” without sourced, dated evidence.
- “Authentic Islamic style” without specific lineage, source, authorship, and review.
