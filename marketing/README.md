# mosque.build marketing library

This folder contains reusable, public-facing material for communities planning mosques and contributors building mosque.build. It is a content source, not a record of product completion.

## Audiences

| Audience | Primary need | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| Mosque founders and committees | Understand the full journey and avoid preventable gaps | [For mosque builders](FOR_MOSQUE_BUILDERS.md) |
| Architects, engineers, planners, and contractors | See where professional work connects to community decisions | [Building considerations](MOSQUE_BUILDING_CONSIDERATIONS.md) |
| Developers, designers, and researchers | Find a responsible contribution path | [Contributor campaign](CONTRIBUTOR_CAMPAIGN.md) |
| Donors and community members | Understand why transparent planning matters | [Messaging guide](MESSAGING.md) |
| Writers, educators, and social teams | Publish accurate, consistent content | [Launch kit](LAUNCH_KIT.md) and [editorial calendar](EDITORIAL_CALENDAR.md) |

- [Messaging](MESSAGING.md) — positioning, benefits, voice, proof, and calls to action.

## Core message

> Building a mosque should not be a mystery.

mosque.build helps a community move from need and site questions through design, approvals, funding, sourcing, construction, opening, and long-term care—without pretending software replaces qualified people.

## Publishing checklist

Before publishing material from this folder:

1. Confirm every product claim against the current application and feature status.
2. Check every historical, legal, financial, technical, and religious claim against its cited source and date.
3. Remove private project details and personal information.
4. Confirm image, font, quotation, and media rights.
5. Use the canonical name `mosque.build` and domain `https://mosque.build`.
6. Keep professional and authority review boundaries visible.
7. Adapt the call to action to the audience and channel.
