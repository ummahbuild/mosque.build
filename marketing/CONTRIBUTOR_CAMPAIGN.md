# Contributor campaign

## Campaign idea

**Help every mosque project begin with a better map.**

mosque.build is open-source infrastructure for communities planning, designing, funding, building, opening, and caring for mosques. The opportunity is larger than a drawing tool: it includes trustworthy data, accessible workflows, community governance, cost evidence, construction coordination, cultural context, and institutional memory.

## Who can contribute

### Developers

Help with Next.js, React, TypeScript, domain modelling, offline behavior, testing, privacy, security, performance, import/export, mapping, and 3D adapters.

### Designers and accessibility specialists

Improve complex planning flows, mobile use, keyboard access, screen-reader structure, data visualization, plain language, error recovery, reduced motion, and inclusive research.

### Built-environment professionals

Architects, engineers, planners, quantity surveyors, project managers, contractors, facilities teams, and authority professionals can review where conceptual prompts need stronger boundaries, evidence, or handoff requirements.

### Researchers and data contributors

Add primary sources with dates, rights, geography, confidence, and review status. Help distinguish a research lead from a verified record.

### Cultural and religious reviewers

Help the project treat mosque lineages, worship spaces, patterns, craft, language, and religious content with context and care. mosque.build does not generate sacred calligraphy as decorative filler.

### Writers and translators

Improve guidance, terminology, onboarding, contributor documentation, and community-facing education without turning incomplete work into marketing claims.

## Starter contribution paths

- Reproduce and fix one mobile or keyboard bug.
- Add an error, empty, stale, or offline state to an existing flow.
- Improve one test so it checks behavior rather than internal wording.
- Review one source record for URL, date, rights, confidence, and geographic scope.
- Improve one planning tool’s plain-language explanation.
- Add a privacy-safe example or accessibility test.
- Review one 3D component for cleanup, reduced motion, and renderer-neutral inputs.
- Document one current route or domain contract.

## Contributor post

What if open-source contributors could help a mosque committee ask better questions before a costly decision?

mosque.build connects community need, site evidence, qibla-aware planning, accessibility, architecture, approvals, cost, procurement, construction, and long-term care.

We welcome developers, designers, architects, engineers, planners, quantity surveyors, facilities teams, researchers, writers, translators, and cultural or religious reviewers.

This is not about replacing professionals. It is about helping communities coordinate them, preserve evidence, and carry decisions across the whole project.

Choose one scoped issue. Keep facts sourced. Use neutral sample data. Add the tests and review gates the work requires.

## Maintainer response promise

Do not publish a response-time commitment unless the maintainers can sustain it. Prefer: “We will triage contributions as maintainer capacity allows and will label work that needs specialist review.”
