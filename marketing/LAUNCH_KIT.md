## Email for mosque communities

**Subject options**

- A clearer way to begin a mosque project
- Before the land, rendering, and fundraiser
- Plan the whole mosque journey

**Body**

Assalamu alaikum,

A mosque project is more than a building. It connects worship, community, land, qibla, accessibility, design, approvals, cost, funding, sourcing, construction, opening, and care.

mosque.build helps a committee organize those decisions in one guided journey. The public workspace begins with a private brief saved in your browser, then connects you to planning tools and practical guides for each stage.

It does not replace architects, engineers, authorities, legal advisers, scholars, or contractors. It helps communities prepare better questions, see what still needs verification, and preserve the reasons behind decisions.

Start here: https://mosque.build/start

Please share feedback using neutral examples rather than private project information.

## LinkedIn launch post

Building a mosque should not be a mystery.

The visible moment may be a rendering or fundraiser. The real project connects:

community need → site → qibla → accessibility → architecture → engineering → approvals → cost → funding → procurement → construction → handover → operations → long-term care

mosque.build brings that journey into one open-source planning workspace.

The aim is not to replace qualified professionals or authorities. It is to help communities prepare better briefs, compare options, preserve evidence, understand the next decision, and carry institutional knowledge from one committee to the next.

Communities can begin with a private browser-based project brief. Contributors can help with code, design, accessibility, research, documentation, translation, data provenance, and professional review.

Explore: https://mosque.build
Contribute: https://github.com/ummahbuild/mosque.build
