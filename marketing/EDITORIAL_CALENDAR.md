## Weekly production pattern

### Monday — question

Publish one question a committee can use immediately. Invite responses without soliciting private project data.

### Wednesday — explanation

Publish the main article, diagram, interview, or product walkthrough. Include sources and boundaries.

### Friday — practical action

Share a checklist or direct route into mosque.build. Avoid presenting Friday prayer audiences as a marketing opportunity without community consent.

### Monthly — contributor update

Summarize merged improvements, open review needs, data provenance work, accessibility progress, and honest limitations. Use feature statuses accurately.

## Measurement

Track useful, privacy-preserving signals rather than vanity alone:

- visits that continue into a project brief or planning guide;
- guide completion and return visits where consented and safely measured;
- contributor-guide visits, issues opened, and pull requests merged;
- accessibility and documentation improvements completed;
- sources reviewed or corrected;
- qualitative feedback from committees and professionals;
- content corrections and time-to-correction.

Do not collect sensitive project details merely to measure marketing performance.
