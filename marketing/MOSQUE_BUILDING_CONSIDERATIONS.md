# Mosque-building considerations

This checklist helps a community prepare questions and evidence. It is not a substitute for local law, site investigation, religious review, professional design, cost advice, construction contracts, or authority approval.

## 1. Purpose and community

- What worship, learning, welfare, family, youth, elder, and community activities must the mosque support?
- What are normal, Friday, Ramadan, Eid, funeral, and emergency operating modes?
- Which needs are supported by evidence, and which remain assumptions?
- How might the community change over ten, twenty, or fifty years?
- Can the project open in useful phases without unsafe or wasteful temporary work?

## 2. Governance and stewardship

- Who recommends, reviews, decides, communicates, and records each major choice?
- How are conflicts of interest declared and managed?
- How are donor restrictions, procurement decisions, and changes recorded?
- Who can commit funds or instruct work?
- How will institutional knowledge survive changes in committee membership?

## 3. Land, property, and existing buildings

- Is ownership and the right to develop or occupy verified?
- Is mosque or assembly use likely to be permitted at this location?
- What surveys, title checks, leases, easements, rights of way, or neighbour agreements are needed?
- Can an existing building safely accommodate qibla, prayer rows, occupancy, fire escape, accessibility, wudu, services, and structural alterations?
- What site constraint could make a low purchase price expensive later?

## 4. Site and climate

- How do topography, soil, groundwater, drainage, flooding, erosion, heat, wind, seismic risk, wildfire, snow, rain, and air quality affect the project?
- Are water, sewer or treatment, power, communications, waste, and emergency services available and adequate?
- How will people arrive by walking, wheelchair, bicycle, public transport, car, service vehicle, and emergency vehicle?
- Can the site support safe queues, deliveries, funeral activity, event peaks, and future work?
- Which conclusions need a surveyor, geotechnical engineer, environmental professional, civil engineer, or authority source?

## 5. Qibla and prayer planning

- What method, source, accuracy, and accountable review establish the project qibla basis?
- How do prayer rows interact with columns, doors, walls, accessible positions, circulation, overflow, and floor finishes?
- Can worshippers align without creating obstacles or unsafe crowd movement?
- How do sound, daylight, glare, temperature, and ventilation change across prayer times and seasons?
- Which questions need local religious and community review?

## 6. Inclusive access and dignity

- Can a disabled worshipper move independently from arrival to entrance, prayer, wudu, toilet, learning, and community spaces?
- Are women’s entrances, prayer spaces, sightlines, sound, amenities, safety, and participation dignified and equitable?
- Are children, elders, carers, converts, neurodivergent people, and people with sensory needs considered?
- Are gradients, surfaces, doors, thresholds, lifts, signage, lighting, acoustics, seating, and emergency evacuation coordinated?
- Has the design been reviewed with people who will use these journeys?

## 7. Wudu, toilets, and water

- Are wet and dry circulation separated where practical?
- Are privacy, dignity, accessibility, slip resistance, drainage, ventilation, cleaning, and maintenance resolved together?
- Can fixtures, traps, drains, valves, pumps, heaters, and waterproofing be safely inspected and repaired?
- What happens during water interruption, drainage blockage, flooding, or high demand?
- Are water demand, storage, reuse, discharge, and public-health requirements supported by local evidence?

## 8. Architecture, structure, and materials

- Does the building form serve worship, climate, site, programme, affordability, and maintenance?
- Are roofs, domes, minarets, arcades, screens, and ornaments connected to a credible structure and weathering strategy?
- Are local materials, skills, repair knowledge, lead times, fire behavior, moisture, acoustics, durability, embodied impact, and replacement considered?
- Can high-level surfaces, lights, gutters, roofs, façades, and equipment be maintained safely?
- Are patterns and craft grounded in a specific source, authorship, application, and cultural review?

## 9. Fire, life safety, and security

- What occupancy classifications, escape widths, travel distances, fire separations, alarms, suppression, emergency lighting, and fire-service access are likely to apply?
- Do normal layouts, prayer rows, Ramadan, events, schools, kitchens, and temporary arrangements preserve safe escape?
- How are safeguarding, privacy, crowd management, violence prevention, and emergency communication addressed without making the mosque hostile?
- Who is authorized to review, test, inspect, and approve each system?

## 10. Acoustics, lighting, energy, and services

- Can speech be understood without harmful sound levels or excessive reverberation?
- Do daylight and electric light support prayer, reading, teaching, cleaning, security, and wayfinding without glare?
- Are ventilation, cooling, heating, humidity, water, drainage, power, data, fire, security, and audio-visual systems coordinated with rooms and structure?
- How do daily prayer cycles and occasional peak events affect energy strategy?
- What continuity plan supports power, cooling, water, communications, and critical safety systems during outages?

## 11. Planning, permits, and approvals

- Which authority controls land use, building, fire, accessibility, traffic, environment, utilities, signage, heritage, and occupancy?
- Are sources current, directly linked, dated, and specific to the site and scope?
- Who owns each submission, response, revision, inspection, condition, and expiry date?
- Which items are likely applicable and still need verification?
- What cannot proceed safely or contractually before approval?

## 12. Cost, funding, and waqf

- Does every cost state geography, currency, date, source, maturity, confidence, exclusions, and contingency?
- Are site work, professional fees, approvals, taxes, escalation, utilities, furniture, audio-visual systems, commissioning, opening, and reserves included?
- How will restricted donations be recorded and reported?
- Does the cash-flow plan match realistic design, approval, procurement, and construction timing?
- Do waqf, charity, financing, and governance structures have qualified jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, governance, and Shariah review?

## 13. Procurement and integrity

- Are scopes complete enough to compare like with like?
- Are supplier identity, technical evidence, samples, warranties, lead time, delivery, installation, taxes, duties, and exclusions verified?
- Are competitive comparison, approval limits, segregation of duties, audit trails, and conflict disclosure proportionate to risk?
- Can substitutions be traced back to design intent and professional review?
- Is progress evidence kept separate from payment approval?

## 14. Construction and handover

- Is the programme connected to design information, approvals, procurement, inspections, and community constraints?
- How are site questions, product submissions, changes, defects, delays, risks, and decisions recorded?
- Are temporary works, sequencing, public protection, worker welfare, and weather resilience planned?
- Does handover include tests, certificates, commissioning, training, warranties, manuals, spares, keys, asset records, and unresolved defects?
- Which records are project-team notes, and which are professional or authority approvals?

## 15. Opening, operations, and resilience

- Who operates, cleans, secures, maintains, and inspects each part of the mosque?
- What are the first-year operating budget and long-term replacement plan?
- Are emergency plans prepared for fire, flood, heatwave, severe weather, power outage, water outage, cooling failure, crowd surge, and communication loss?
- Are staff and volunteers trained, and are accessible alternatives available during disruption?
- How will post-occupancy feedback become accountable improvements?

## A useful decision record

For each major choice, record the need, options, evidence, source date, assumptions, trade-offs, responsible role, reviewers, decision, affected cost and programme, next action, and review date.
