# Architecture feature integration plan

## Outcome

Integrate both supplied 100-feature architecture briefs as a traceable 200-proposal delivery system, not a list of implied capabilities. The sources are preserved in `apps/web/data/architecture-feature-plan.json` and `apps/web/data/modern-architecture-feature-plan.json`; every proposal retains a stable identifier, category, intended outcome, renderer-adapter concept and provenance dated 2026-09-10.

## Product integration delivered

- A searchable and filterable 200-feature planner in the Design workspace, with core and modern-performance collection filters.
- Project selection with `now`, `next` and `later` priorities.
- Independent delivery status using `concept | specified | scaffolded | executable | source_backed | beta | production_ready`.
- Accountable role, evidence/issue reference and acceptance-note fields.
- Per-feature unit, integration, accessibility, responsive, provenance and human-review test prompts.
- Device-local persistence and whole-project package import/export.
- Workflow dependency on project brief and site analysis.
- Renderer neutrality: Three.js identifiers describe adapter responsibilities, never domain objects.
- Explicit professional, community and religious-content review gates.

## Cross-module delivery sequence

### Phase A — shared analysis contracts

Deliver typed, bounded domain contracts for overlays, scenario states, spatial samples, evidence and uncertainty before new rendering. Prioritize FEATURE-001–010 because terrain, qibla confidence, flood, wind, noise, arrival, privacy and neighbor impact become inputs to later envelope, program and lifecycle work.

Acceptance: portable schemas; unit and import validation; no precise-location assumption; source metadata; uncertainty rendering; accessible non-WebGL summary; qualified site review.

### Phase B — worship and inclusive programming

Connect FEATURE-011–030 to the room program, women’s-space study, social architecture, prayer occupancy, floor plan and opening model. Build scenario presets for daily, Friday, Ramadan, Eid, after-hours and retreat use. Replace simplistic parity “scores” with transparent comparable measures and consultation evidence.

Acceptance: mode switching does not overwrite base geometry; accessible positions stay within rows; capacities expose entered assumptions; flow and sightline results state limitations; community and professional review are recorded.

### Phase C — sacred core and purification

Connect FEATURE-031–050 to architectural components, building systems, interiors, acoustics and wudu planning. Calligraphy remains a reviewed content zone rather than generated decoration. Water, drainage, transfer, privacy, pressure and cleaning studies remain conceptual until locally reviewed.

Acceptance: sacred-content provenance and review; renderer-neutral service zones; bounded water arithmetic; dry-route and splash geometry tests; plumbing/accessibility/acoustic review gates.

### Phase D — envelope, climate, sound and light

Connect FEATURE-051–080 to climate resilience, solar/daylight, roof, building systems, interiors and energy scenarios. Use time-stamped scenarios rather than claiming calibrated prediction. Couple operable states to energy, emergency and maintenance records.

Acceptance: source-backed weather/input provenance; comparison against a baseline; reduced-motion rendering; glare/audio/privacy failure states; maintainability access; professional commissioning boundary.

### Phase E — fabrication, construction and operation

Connect FEATURE-081–099 to material selection, BIM mappings, construction sequencing, cost controls, integrity controls, asset handover and work orders. Waste estimates must retain quantity basis, geography, date, source, maturity, confidence and exclusions.

Acceptance: stable element and shop IDs; tolerance and sequence checks; no structural or safety approval claims; evidence-gated installation state; asset lifecycle linkage; offline conflict tests; RLS tests before cloud collaboration.

### Phase F — natural-language operations

FEATURE-100 remains `specified` until commands are constrained to a typed, previewable and reversible operation vocabulary. No generated instruction may silently change geometry, approve regulated work, invent evidence, or bypass review.

Acceptance: allowlisted commands; preview/diff/confirm; undo; authorization and RLS tests; prompt-injection tests; provenance for generated proposals; deterministic fallback; professional review for regulated effects.

## Delivery rules

1. Advance status only when the implementation and declared tests exist.
2. Keep canonical geometry and project data renderer-neutral.
3. Treat simulations as scenario comparisons until calibrated and reviewed.
4. Do not infer laws, prices, product availability, approvals or religious positions.
5. Every external dataset needs provenance, date, rights, confidence and review state.
6. Every regulated output needs a named human review gate and unresolved-state behavior.
7. Provide keyboard and touch operation, text alternatives and mobile parity.
8. Preserve user edits, stable identifiers, import validation and reversible changes.
9. Require RLS and security tests before project/member data reaches a shared backend.
10. Link contributor work to a feature ID and acceptance evidence in the exported plan.
