## Evidence and review boundary

All ground descriptions, dimensions, bearing and settlement values, loads, foundation/system options, hazard states, temporary-works notes and hold-point releases are user-entered records. Qualified geotechnical and structural professionals must establish investigation scope, the ground model, governing criteria, analysis, load combinations, robustness, fire and durability strategy, details, temporary works, inspections and acceptance. Verify construction safety and authority obligations in the project jurisdiction.
