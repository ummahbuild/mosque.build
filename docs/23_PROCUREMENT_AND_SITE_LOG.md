# Procurement and site-update workspaces

Status: `executable` device-local workflows with `source_backed` procurement seeds.

The marketplace route now supports search across an 18-scope mosque needs catalog, category filters, region-aware live search handoffs to Amazon plus global Temu, eBay and Alibaba searches, comparison of up to three observed product records, project-scope selection and versioned JSON scope-brief export. The needs catalog contains repository-authored specification prompts, not listings or product claims. Product records preserve source URL, observation date, verification status and price scope where a price was observed. A listing or marketplace result is never presented as availability, recommendation, technical approval or a current quotation.

The landed-cost workspace accepts up to six user-entered quotes. Every comparison record requires a supplier or quote reference, geography, currency, observation date, positive base amount, exclusions and responsible reviewer role. Base, freight, duty/import, tax, installation and other entered allowances remain separately visible. Mixed currencies trigger a warning and are never ranked or converted. Source type, maturity, confidence, validity date and project-team review state travel in the versioned export and whole-project package.

The construction route now supports factual site-update capture, area/type classification, reported-progress input, evidence references, accountable review status, filtering and empty states. Records remain local to the browser. They are not instructions, certified progress, inspections, payment evidence or an audit-grade server record.

Offline field snapshots make that local constraint explicit and manageable. The construction workspace exports a versioned, bounded snapshot and accepts only strictly shaped JSON records. Incoming-only, identical and colliding records are previewed separately; every collision must be resolved as local, incoming or a separate copy before the merge can be applied. No import silently overwrites a local update, and the merge history remains explicitly non-tamper-proof.

Both workflows require RLS-backed project storage, permissions, server audit history and integration tests before authenticated collaboration can move beyond `scaffolded` status.

The operations route now continues handover into a device-local asset register. It records asset and room references without requesting addresses or personal names, separates project-team commissioning state from statutory approval, calculates deterministic service dates only from user-entered dates and intervals, and exports the full review boundary. It does not provide manufacturer instructions, verified warranties, statutory inspection evidence or notifications.

Asset-linked work orders extend the same local record into reported maintenance needs, priorities, target dates and evidence-gated closure. A closed task remains a project-team state: it does not certify an inspection, technical adequacy, payment entitlement or safe return to service.

Six climate emergency operating modes now turn the saved resilience study into explicit preparedness questions for heat, flood, smoke, storms, water scarcity and compound utility disruption. Each records roles rather than personal contacts, accountable warning sources, accessible and offline communication routes, response/recovery prompts and drills. The workspace never issues a live alert or substitutes for emergency services, authority guidance or a locally approved emergency plan.

The people privacy planner keeps volunteer, staff and contractor preparation at role level. It records purpose, minimum access scopes, data-owner role, retention-review date, privacy and safeguarding review states and external evidence references while explicitly excluding direct identifiers and sensitive case data. Personal rosters and safeguarding or employment records require a secured, permissioned system governed under applicable local requirements.
