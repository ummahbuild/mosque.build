# Climate resilience workflow

Status: `executable` for user-directed scenario planning; not a hazard assessment.

The design workspace now covers extreme heat, intense rain and surface flooding, drought and water scarcity, smoke and hazardous outdoor air, severe wind and storms, coastal flooding and salt exposure, and compound utility disruption. Users classify each as not assessed, monitor or priority; the interface reports coverage, flags interacting priority hazards, separates design checks from operating checks, saves review notes and retains IPCC provenance.

The workflow does not infer risk from a location or provide a climate forecast. Project teams must use current official local hazard data, suitable future-weather files, applicable requirements, emergency-management guidance and qualified review.
