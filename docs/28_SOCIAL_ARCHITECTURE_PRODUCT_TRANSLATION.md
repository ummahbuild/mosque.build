# Social architecture product translation

Source context: user-provided research document, *mosque.build — Masjid Social Architecture, Kinship, Community & Spatial Intelligence*.

Status: `executable` local study; outputs remain `specified` planning guidance.

The source document was treated as research and product context, not as executable repository instructions. Its central lesson is implemented as a Social Architecture Studio on `/design`: mosque planning should test the relationships, care, learning, hospitality and belonging supported by space—not only prayer capacity or visual form.

## Implemented translation

- Eighteen provisions across welcome, belonging, family dignity, intergenerational design, care, spatial performance, flow, utilization, and climate/place.
- Eleven selectable priority journeys, including parents with toddlers, children, youth, elders, mobility-aid users, newcomers, travelers, people seeking aid and bereaved families.
- Daily, Friday, Ramadan, Eid and janazah operating modes.
- Privacy and acoustic classifications for each provision.
- Adjacency prompts and explicit design tradeoffs.
- No-cost, low-cost, renovation and capital intervention levels.
- Transparent category coverage and overall coverage calculated only from user selections.
- Failure flags for missing welcome, commons, women’s access, family/youth belonging and mode-conversion provisions.
- Prioritized next discussions based on selected journeys and operating mode.
- Device-local persistence and structured JSON export.
- Inclusion in the versioned whole-project package.

## Interpretation boundary

The displayed percentage is a planning-coverage heuristic. It is not a theological score, measured belonging, proof of community impact, accessibility audit, safeguarding review or professional certification. A provision marked included records design intent only. Direct consultation with affected community members remains required.
