# mosque.build — Additional Video Concepts & Scripts

## Video 1 — “5 mistakes before buying mosque land” (4–6 min)

**Hook:**  
The cheapest mosque land can become the most expensive land you ever buy.

### Mistake 1 — confirming ownership but not use
A clean title does not answer whether the intended mosque/community-center use is allowed.

### Mistake 2 — parking afterthought
Friday peak, Eid, Ramadan and school pickup are not normal weekday occupancy.

### Mistake 3 — ignoring utilities
Water, sewer, electricity, drainage and road access can create major off-site costs.

### Mistake 4 — designing before site constraints
Setbacks, fire access, flood, topography and stormwater can shrink the usable building envelope.

### Mistake 5 — no future phase
Cheap land loses its advantage if every square meter is consumed by the first building.

**CTA:**  
mosque.build should turn this into a land due-diligence checklist before a community commits.
