# Mosque emergency continuity plan

Status: `executable` planning workflow; not a live alerting or emergency-command system.

## Operating-state model

Every scenario should produce an explicit, accountable building state: normal operation, reduced service, temporary refuge, closed, or recovery. A backup system is not sufficient by itself: the team must record which services it supports, for how long, under which conditions, who may operate it, and which failure requires closure.

## Conditions now implemented

1. Power outage
2. Cooling or ventilation failure
3. Extreme heat
4. Water outage or scarcity
5. Drainage or sanitation failure
6. Fire, smoke or alarm activation
7. Earthquake or ground movement
8. Flooding or intense rain
9. Smoke or hazardous outdoor air
10. Severe wind, lightning or storm
11. Extreme cold or heating failure
12. Communications or internet outage
13. Compound disaster or utility disruption

Each condition includes activation evidence, linked system dependencies, immediate operating decisions, stop/closure conditions, return-to-service checks, an accountable lead role, accessible primary and offline communication, notes, and drill evidence.

## Integration sequence

- Design: connect critical loads, passive refuge, water and sanitation, accessible routes, alarms, structure, roof, drainage and communications.
- Procurement: require evidence for backup-system compatibility, runtime, maintenance, safe installation and end-of-life care rather than assuming availability.
- Construction and commissioning: test failure modes and record witnessed results against accepted plans.
- Opening: assign roles, publish accessible messages, establish offline records and run tabletop plus physical drills.
- Operations: inspect supplies and systems, repeat drills, record failures, update dependencies and retain professional/authority review.

## Next production gates

- Link emergency dependencies to actual asset-register IDs and rooms.
- Link critical loads to the energy scenario without inventing runtime.
- Link water and sanitation dependencies to wudu and building-systems records.
- Link fire, accessible egress and powered-door behavior to reviewed opening records.
- Add signed-off commissioning evidence, expiry dates and role-based access.
- Add a printable offline plan after privacy, accessibility, fire and emergency-management review.
- Add source-backed jurisdiction overlays only where current official evidence is available.
- Run accessibility, mobile/offline, RLS/security and professional review tests before production-ready status.

## Boundary

mosque.build does not issue live alerts, predict hazards, direct emergency response, verify credentials or declare a building safe. Follow the approved local emergency plan, current official warnings, emergency services, responsible authorities and qualified professionals.
