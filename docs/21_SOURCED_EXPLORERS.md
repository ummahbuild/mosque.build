# Sourced design, permit and funding explorers

Status: `source_backed` reference data with `executable` local interactions.

This release adds jurisdiction selectors for permits and funding/endowment starting points, using records observed on 2026-08-21 from official authorities and regulators. Every item names the authority and source and retains the required language: likely applicable, verify with authority or professional. No record represents an approval, complete legal checklist, fee quote or current availability.

The pattern library now describes documented Islamic architectural and geometric families from Metropolitan Museum of Art and Victoria and Albert Museum educational or collection context. Displayed diagrams are original CSS interpretations. They are not replicas, sacred calligraphy, fabrication drawings or claims of regional authenticity. Historical context, source links, rights notes and specialist review prompts are visible for each study.

Funding records distinguish ordinary campaigns, restricted funds, reserves, permanent endowment and waqf review. The interface does not classify an arrangement as waqf; qualified legal, governance, tax and Shariah review remains mandatory.

Verification includes data-integrity tests, TypeScript, production build, mobile and desktop browser checks, dropdown state changes, source links, design shortlisting, guide links, overflow and console inspection.
