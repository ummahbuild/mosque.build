# Advanced interior and prayer-floor planning

Status: `executable` device-local design and procurement workflow.

Each schematic room now has a floor strategy, transition intent, maintenance-zone decision and notes. Carpet rooms add editable prayer-row module, candidate roll width and planning allowance. Deterministic takeoff prompts expose base area, allowance area, geometric row-line count and roll lengths across the provisional room width. These are not measured quantities, capacity calculations, orders or quotations.

A dedicated Three.js interior adapter renders semantic finish surfaces and conceptual prayer-row lines from the saved study, with orbit controls, responsive sizing, reduced-motion handling and resource cleanup. A paired SVG plan provides an accessible overview and direct room selection.

The Marketplace handoff preloads the prayer-carpet need, calculated planning scope and evidence checklist. Live platform results remain unverified. The versioned `mosque.build/interior-study@1` record also travels in the whole-project package.
