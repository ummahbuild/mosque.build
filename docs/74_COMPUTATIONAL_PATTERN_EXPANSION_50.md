## Product integration

All 50 studies participate in library search/category filtering, local shortlisting, the editable pattern-placement workflow, 3D line geometry, surface/depth/repetition controls, project persistence and JSON/GLB export. Deterministic rendering is bounded to 5,000 segments. Catalog IDs are allowlisted during restore.

## Review and next gates

Each record carries conceptual provenance, original-geometry rights language, application intent and three review prompts. Before use, teams must resolve authorship, local cultural context, border conditions, tolerance, substrate, fixing, fire, accessibility, weather, daylight/privacy, cleaning, replacement and specialist acceptance. True fabrication geometry, robust polygon clipping, structural/open-area analysis, version history and authenticated collaboration remain future work.
