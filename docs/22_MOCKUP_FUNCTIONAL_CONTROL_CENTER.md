# Mockup-derived project control center

Status: `executable` local planning workspace.

The repeated interaction patterns across the design, permit, funding and construction mockups now run inside `/my-project` as a connected control center. State is stored on the device under `mosque-build.control-center.v1`.

Implemented interactions:

- choose and persist one of three design-planning scenarios;
- switch permit jurisdiction and mark sourced prompts reviewed;
- allocate six budget-purpose percentages with a 100% balance indicator;
- record six lifecycle phase-progress inputs;
- preserve a timestamped local activity log;
- continue into sourced patterns, permits, funding and construction routes.

Truth boundaries are part of the interface. Scenario cards are not architecture. Permit review is not submission or approval. Budget percentages are not costs or quotations. Phase inputs are not certified progress or payment evidence. Server collaboration, audit-grade records and regulated decisions remain separate future work with RLS and professional review requirements.

Verification: unit suite, repository validation, TypeScript, production build, mobile and desktop browser interaction, overflow inspection, console inspection and framework-overlay inspection.
