## Review boundary

All dimensions, ratings, operation states and review statuses are user records. Current jurisdiction-specific accessibility, egress, fire, security, automated-gate, structural, electrical and weather requirements must be established from dated official evidence and reviewed by qualified professionals and authorities. Tested assemblies, manufacturer instructions, installation, commissioning and maintenance remain part of the handoff.
