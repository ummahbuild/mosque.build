# Community needs and service-area study

Status: `executable`

This module helps a project team estimate the scale and mix of service without building a list of Muslims or households. It accepts aggregate service-area population, explicit low/base/high assumptions, likely project participation, evidence status, source title, public URL and observation date. Anonymous survey summaries can inform prayer, education, care and recreation scenarios.

## Privacy rules

- Never collect names, addresses, contact details, device identifiers, exact coordinates, household rows or inferred religious identity in this module.
- Use voluntary, non-coercive and accessible engagement with translated formats where appropriate.
- Do not publish segments smaller than the chosen minimum reporting group.
- Record sampling, non-response, access and digital-exclusion limitations.
- Keep raw survey governance outside the public planning export and follow applicable privacy law with qualified review.

## Interpretation

Outputs are scenarios—not census results, demographic truth, demand forecasts, religious-membership findings, capacity approvals or code calculations. Population and participation percentages are user-entered assumptions. Each public statistic must be verified at its original source and dated.

## Integration

The larger of the Friday and Eid working scenarios can be sent to the saved campus plan as its working peak and mosque-zone capacity. This remains an editable assumption and must be reconciled with the room programme, prayer-row study, transport plan, phased construction strategy and community review.
