## Why the indicators are not a health score

The current values are transparent comparison heuristics derived only from the visible inputs. They do not claim to measure the quality, Islamic validity or real-world performance of a mosque. A stronger assessment would require sourced criteria, calibrated evidence, named reviewers, accessibility testing and post-occupancy validation.
