# Versioned room program

Status: `executable` device-local architecture workflow.

The design workspace now connects generated schematic rooms to an editable brief: purpose, priority, privacy gradient, acoustic and daylight intent, continuous accessible-journey requirement, desired adjacency, desired separation, review state and notes. Coverage exposes missing relationship and review decisions without claiming design quality or compliance.

Users can retain up to twelve named alternative snapshots, remove them independently, export a versioned `mosque.build/room-program@1` record and carry it inside the whole-project package. Geometry remains renderer-neutral and unchanged by requirement editing. Area is a provisional schematic quantity, not measured capacity or a professional calculation.
